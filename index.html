    imm[12|10:5] lists the immediate bits held by a field, MSB first. rd′/rs1′/rs2′ encode x8–x15 (f8–f15).

    Encodings checked against the RISC-V Unprivileged ISA manual (v20260120): Ch. 36 instruction listings (RV32I, Zifencei, Zicsr, M, A, F, D), RVC listing figures (C) and per-instruction encodings (Zicond, Zba/Zbb/Zbc/Zbs). The privileged groups (Priv, Smrnmi, Smctr, Svinval, H) were checked against the Vol. II Ch. 19 privileged instruction listings. F/D register fields name f-registers where the syntax says fd/fs1/…; hover over a mnemonic or its syntax for a description of the instruction, and over a field to see its bit range and value. Append ?ext=RV32I,M,C to the URL to open with only those extensions shown, and &view=op for the opcode view (plus &by=f3, f7 or f37 to group by funct3/funct7 as well). In the opcode view, C instructions are grouped by their RVC opcode-map cell (funct3 + op); the search field also accepts an opcode such as op-fp, 0x33 or 0b0110011.
