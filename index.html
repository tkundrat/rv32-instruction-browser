    meta marks a meta-extension: its checkbox selects all of its member extensions. A member that belongs to several meta-extensions (e.g. Zca in C and Zce) appears under each of them.

    Encodings checked against the RISC-V Unprivileged and Privileged ISA manuals (v20260120): the instruction-set listings, the RVC listing figures, the per-instruction encoding diagrams, and the vector instruction listing / load-store tables. Encodings of the extensions added from the riscv/riscv-opcodes repository were cross-checked against those manuals wherever the manual contains a textual encoding. Extensions without new instruction encodings are not listed: Zicntr/Zihpm (CSR-read pseudo-instructions), Zmmul (subset of M), Ztso, Zfinx/Zdinx/Zhinx (reuse the F/D/Zfh encodings with x registers), Zkr/Zkt, Zvkt, Zve*, and RV32E. Hover over a mnemonic or its syntax for a description, and over a field for its bit range and value. URL parameters: ?ext=G,B,Zce (extensions or meta-extensions), &view=op, &by=f3|f7|f37.
